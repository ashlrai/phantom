Original `src/cli/intent.ts` exported `cmdIntent` with list, get, add, update, multi-file import, stale, link and ship. Master registers `phm fleet intent` in `src/cli/fleet-subcommand-registry.ts`; `src/cli/fleet-intent.ts` implements those verbs, delegates edit and import, and calls the ledger/read, sweep and evidence operations. `test/fleet-intent-unified-lifecycle-09sqk1.test.ts` exercises add, update, link, ship, get and list, including evidence replay and invalid status refusal. `test/intent-guide-reconcile-09spl1.test.ts` covers the separate import, read, evidence, summary and sweep commands.
