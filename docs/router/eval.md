# Router eval harness

`phm eval router --dataset jobs.jsonl [--policy NAME] [--decisions] [--json] [--write report.json] [--check baseline.json]` replays a router or prompt policy over a frozen set of past router lines with known outcomes and reports merge rate, cost per merged PR and wall time with confidence intervals. It reads local files only and makes no network requests. `--policy list` prints the builtin policies (`logged`, `strict-pool`, `cheapest`, `hold-all`); any other policy is supplied through the `RouterPolicy` seam in `src/core/eval/router-eval.ts`.

The dataset is router-eval.v1 JSONL. An optional first `{"v":1,"kind":"meta"}` line carries the arm catalog (`catalogCostUsd`, `tier`, `label`) — decision-time knowledge only. Each job line records the job id, the router-time fields (`repo`, `pool`, `tier`, `priority`, `task`), the recorded eligible `candidates`, the `chosen` arm (null only for `held`), the `outcome` (`merged`, `closed`, `failed`, `held`, `pending`), and measured `costUsd`, `tokens`, `wallTimeMs`. An `arms` object may freeze per-candidate counterfactual outcomes for benchmark lines; an entry for the arm that ran must agree with the recorded outcome. Duplicate ids, chosen arms outside `candidates`, non-terminal counterfactuals and contradictory records are rejected with a line number — never silently replayed.

A replayed decision matching the recorded arm observes the recorded outcome; a changed decision observes its frozen counterfactual when one exists and is otherwise *unobserved* — never estimated or credited. Held and pending lines stay out of the terminal cohort, and a decision outside the recorded candidates is rejected and counted. Merge rate carries a Wilson score interval; cost-per-merged-PR and wall-time means carry seeded percentile bootstrap intervals (the seed is pinned in the report so a baseline reproduces byte for byte). Intervals describe sampling noise over the frozen set, not counterfactual certainty.

`--check` exits 1 when the replay drifts from a baseline report, naming the metric that moved. `benchmarks/router-eval/` holds the committed frozen day, baseline report and regeneration commands; `test/router-eval-harness.test.ts` runs the gate in the daily master run.
